Civic reporting platform for the community of Osorno, Chile.

Report potholes, faulty streetlights, illegal waste dumps, and sinkholes across the 951 km² of the municipality (urban and rural sectors).

Features:
- Real-time community map with OpenStreetMap.
- Inaction counter for municipal delays.
- Formal civic complaints generator under Chilean Laws N° 18.695 and N° 19.880 for Contraloría.
- 100 % tracker-free and ad-free.
